First comment (maker story):
Mirage is a small desert platformer. The spring on the mesa has gone quiet and the town of Dry Creek is out of water. Wren takes Elder Pina's walking staff and goes looking for the cause.

It runs inside Joshua Tree, a computer I am building from scratch. The game's source lives in the Joshua Tree repo, so the world and the story are the same code that runs on that machine. The web page builds the same C to WebAssembly and runs it on a canvas right under the tagline, with no frame around it.

There are five rooms and about ten minutes: Dry Creek, Red Canyon, the Cholla Caves, the Salt Flats and the Mesa. The pixel art, the characters and the story are all ours. Nothing is borrowed.

Duke plays it to the end. Joshua Tree's checks replay that run and must finish in the same spot on the host and inside the OS, so a change to the machine shows up as a failed check.

Keys are A and D or the arrows to walk, W or Up to jump, Space to swing the staff, and S, Down or Enter to talk. On a phone the page shows buttons instead.

It is MIT licensed, and there is a 20 second ad in the repo if you want to see it moving. Tell me what you think in the comments.
